The **Apex Task Chair** is a budget-oriented option that prioritises a small footprint over adjustability. It works for short sessions but shows its limits over a full workday.

## Room dynamics and layout

A compact base makes this the easiest chair to fit into a tight room. Score: **9/10**.

## Customisation and adaptability

Height adjustment only; no lumbar, no recline lock, no armrest movement. Score: **4/10**.

## Comfort for the session

The firm foam seat grows uncomfortable after an hour, and there is no lumbar support. Score: **6/10**.

## Power and practicality

No built-in routing; leads trail across the floor. Score: **5/10**.

## Verdict

A serviceable, affordable starter chair for occasional use — not recommended for extended viewing or work sessions.
