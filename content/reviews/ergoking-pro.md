The **ErgoKing Pro** targets long-duration desk work with a fully adjustable mesh design. It is the most comfortable chair in this roundup, at the cost of a larger footprint.

## Comfort for the session

The headrest, lumbar support, and 4D armrests combine into the best seating posture tested. Score: **10/10**.

## Build and stability

Heavy-duty casters and a reinforced base show no wobble even under load. Score: **9/10**.

## Customisation and adaptability

Nearly every contact point adjusts, and replacement parts are catalogued by the manufacturer. Score: **9/10**.

## Room dynamics and layout

The wide base and recline sweep need generous clearance — a poor fit for compact rooms. Score: **6/10**.

## Verdict

Best-in-class comfort for dedicated home offices, provided you have the room for it.
